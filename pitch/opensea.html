Bridg figures are net of Bridg's platform fee. They use Bridg's best fully-priced route, leaving out venues whose Bridg quote omits a fee charged on top or can't be independently verified (Allbridge, Meson, Husher, Rhino, Eco). Not counted on either side: source-chain gas the user pays in the native token. OpenSea showed a 0% OpenSea fee at the time.
